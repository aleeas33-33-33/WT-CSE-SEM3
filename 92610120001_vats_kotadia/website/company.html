Anduril

Autonomous systems for defence — sensing towers, counter-drone interceptors and the software layer that ties them into existing command structures.

Visit website Related memos

Why we invested

In 2017 the assumption was that defence software would be sold to primes, not around them. Anduril's team argued the opposite: that owning manufacture and selling directly to the end user was the only way to compress a procurement cycle measured in decades.

That was the constraint we wanted named. Nine years later the second procurement door exists, and the companies that built factories were the ones able to walk through it.
