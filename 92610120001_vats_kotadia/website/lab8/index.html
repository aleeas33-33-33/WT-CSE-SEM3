Vision & Mission

What guides the Department of Computer Engineering

V

Vision

To become a globally recognized center of excellence in education, research and innovation by developing skilled, ethical and responsible professionals.

M

Mission

  • ✓ Provide quality education through innovative teaching methods.
  • ✓ Promote practical learning, research and industry interaction.
  • ✓ Develop professional and ethical values among students.

Program Specific Outcomes

After completing the programme, students will be able to:

Apply computer engineering knowledge to analyse and solve real-world problems.

Design, develop and test software using sound programming practices.

Use modern technologies, frameworks and tools effectively in projects.

Work effectively in teams and in professional environments with ethical values.
