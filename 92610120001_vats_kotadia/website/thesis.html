Investment thesis · 2026

Software stopped being the hard part. Everything under it became the hard part.

For fifteen years the scarce input was engineering talent. It is now compute, energy, supply chain and regulatory access. We invest where those constraints bind, because that is where a company that clears them is difficult to copy.

01

Frontier compute

Model labs, accelerators and the data platforms underneath them. The winners will be decided by capital access and distribution, not by architecture papers.

Anthropic Databricks
02

Hard infrastructure

Launch, autonomy and defence. Companies that own a factory as well as a repository, where each delivered unit lowers the cost of the next.

SpaceX Anduril
03

Money and work rails

Payments, credit, payroll and commerce logistics — in the markets adding a billion consumers, not only the ones that already have rails.

Stripe Deel Zepto CRED

What earns a first cheque

  • A constraint you can name

    Founders who can state precisely what is scarce in their market, and why they get it before anyone else.

  • Evidence over narrative

    A shipped product, a signed customer, a working prototype. One of the three, at any stage.

  • A decade of intent

    These markets do not resolve in three years. We underwrite founders planning to still be there in ten.

Where we don't invest

  • Thin application wrappers

    Products whose defensibility is a prompt and a landing page.

  • Roll-ups and arbitrage

    Good businesses. Not venture-shaped, and not ours to run.

  • Anything needing a new consumer habit

    We have been wrong on this category too often to keep paying for the lesson.
